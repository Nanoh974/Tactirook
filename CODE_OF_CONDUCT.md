# Code of conduct

Tactirook welcomes people of all backgrounds and levels of chess or programming
experience. This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews and
other project-managed spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome beginners, accept feedback and acknowledge mistakes.
- Respect privacy, authorship and accessibility needs.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, personal
attacks, doxxing and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting concerns

Contact [the maintainer](https://github.com/Solenia13) through a contact method
they have made public on their GitHub profile. If no private method is available,
use the repository's question/contact issue form to request a private channel:
write only that you need private contact, without names, accusations or incident
details. Do not publish someone else's personal information.

GitHub's own report/block tools can also be used for platform abuse or immediate
privacy concerns. Security vulnerabilities have a separate
[private reporting process](./SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, request a change in behavior,
restrict participation or block an account. Responses depend on severity,
context and repeated behavior. Reports will be handled as discreetly as
practical, without promising confidentiality beyond the project's control.

This is a volunteer project; no fixed moderation response time is guaranteed.
